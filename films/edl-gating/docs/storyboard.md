## What changed in v5

- A small contact offset complements the earlier drawing-order fix.
- Channel electrons span the full path; the plate has reduced glare.
- Cations are amber, anions green and positive gate charges violet-gray, with corresponding legend entries.
- A front-third electrolyte cutaway and interface grid separate overlapping regions; the macro view uses fewer ions.
- The 2D-to-3D transition adds anticipation, acceleration, moving light and coordinated sound.
- Breakdown gains a branching channel, cracks, light pulse and camera emphasis. These are designed cues, not numerical results.
- Shorter text and faster middle/later intervals reduce the roughly 176 s cut to approximately 146 s; the pulse is 104 BPM.
